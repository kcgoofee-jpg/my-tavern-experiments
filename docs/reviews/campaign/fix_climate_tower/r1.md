## Finding
- The Cycles finals look right: no white block and no pink strip is visible in c1, c2 or c3.
- The defect is in the glb. The `props_lights` group (all aether light strips: shaft strips, platform-edge light tubes, the vane lamps and a flat disc under the crown at z = top + 4.1) uses the emissive materials
  `ct_aether_line` (strength 4.0) and `ct_aether_dim` (1.5). Baking emission at that strength clips every texel to pure white: the extracted atlas of `props_lights` is solid white.
  In the viewer these meshes show as plain white: the disc under the crown reads as a white block on top, and the platform-edge light tubes as a bright strip on the podium (the pink tint the user saw is the viewer's light on that white, and is not a material in the model: no pink colour is defined anywhere in the build script).
- The crystal atlas is also washed out to very pale blue for the same reason (strength 9), but it does not read as a block.

## Defects (addressed in the fix stage)
1. Emission strength 4.0 / 1.5 on the light strips bakes to pure white; keep the hue by lowering the strength so the bake stays cyan.
2. The flat glow disc under the crown is hidden in every render but visible in the glb; give it the dim material.

## Not verified
The built-in browser pane has no working WebGL here (the viewer drew only its hotspot markers), so the viewer itself could not be screenshotted; the diagnosis rests on the extracted baked textures and the build script.
