## Fidelity

Present and readable: fortress-square outline (R1), silver-grey ring wall with merlons and walk (R3), eight towers with
silver caps and beacons (R4), keep hall + great round tower with silver crown ring, dark cone and masts (R5), south
gatehouse with twin towers and half-raised portcullis (R6), bridge-head inspection platform with barrier posts and light
gates (C4, R7), patrol-craft pad with three parked craft plus two hangars (C5, R9, R10), sortie pad with beacon ring and
parked hover mounts (C3, R11), gravel drill yard (R2), faint ward (R13), inverted-pyramid plinth with sharp arrises
(R14), four arris emitters = four cores with aether veins (R15, R16), no waterfall / roots (R17), no figures, weapons,
flags or text (R18).

Weak: the corner bulges of the outline (R1 "四角略凸") barely read; rim crystals (R12) are pale white shards with little
cold-blue glow and their board anchor is occluded; the plinth anchor sits inside the rock and is dropped.

## Defects (all to fix in `fix`)

1. **Garrison too small for C2.** ~1200 knights + ~5000 support staff, but the walls enclose mostly empty lawn with eight
   short single-row ranges. Two large lawn quadrants (north-east behind the keep, south-west by the mount hangars) read
   as an empty park, not a headquarters. Add double-row barracks / staff quarters, an armoury-and-stores block, a
   refectory and stables so the enclosure is built up; keep open ground only where it has a use (yard, pads, parade).
2. **Ground reads as a lawn.** The whole interior is green turf. A fortress bailey is paved or gravel with only small
   grass patches; switch the default ground to hard standing and keep turf as patches.
3. **Wall joints.** The 96 wall segments are butt-jointed boxes without mitres: faint vertical seams every few metres
   along the curtain in the main view. Overlap the segments.
4. **Underside rock pattern.** The strata bands are high-contrast "zebra" waves on top of large Voronoi cells, so the
   plinth faces look like a tiled texture instead of dressed grey rock. Lower the strata contrast (closer base colours)
   and keep the faces calm (R14 "面平").
5. **Rim crystals (R12).** Emission too weak to read cold blue; raise it and re-anchor the crystal item outside the ward.
6. **Anchors.** Move the `u_plinth` anchor just outside the plinth face and the `crystal` anchor to the crystal tip so
   the board shows all 15 items.
