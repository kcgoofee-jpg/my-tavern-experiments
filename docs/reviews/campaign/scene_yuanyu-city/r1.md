## What matches
- A walled district with a twin-tower east gate, a round plaza with concentric paving rings and a ring of alternating black and white robed statues, a spire quarter to the north with steep slate roofs and slender spires, a dome quarter to the south with copper and white domes, blue-stone ground and warm street lamps.

## Defects (each one is addressed in the fix stage)
1. The blocks form a chessboard of near-identical boxes at one height; the district looks like a model, not a dense old city.
2. The statues are cones with a ball on top: no shoulders, cowl or flared hem, so they do not read as robed figures. The giant statue gallery of the setting (statues on colonnades) is missing apart from the ring.
3. The east gate is a plain slab with a rectangular hole; the setting's Gothic character asks for a pointed arch and buttresses.
4. Everything is one cold blue: sky, ground beyond the wall and stone; the warm lamp light barely registers.
5. The ground outside the wall is a pale blank plane.

## Not a defect
Neutral by design: no people, text, signs, religious symbols or weapons; statues have no faces and hold nothing.
