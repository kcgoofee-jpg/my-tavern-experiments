## Defects (polish only, not blocking)
1. The black bands read as flat navy stripes; a thin gold edge or a slight relief would make them read as inlaid basalt.
2. The side pavilions have plain white outer walls with no windows, visible from the west camera.
3. The terrace statues are tiny from every camera; a larger scale or a camera on the terrace would show the robed figures.
4. The portico is hollow: no door leaf or lit interior behind the columns beyond a few window blocks.
5. Background towers are pale featureless boxes; a darker tint would help the white building stand out.

## Not a defect
Neutral by design: no people, no text or emblems, no religious symbols, no spear finials (`docs/rejected.md` #5, #6).
