## Defects (polish only, not blocking)
1. The whole image is bright and low-contrast; rings and crystals are plain white, so the "cool cyan-blue light" of the
   setting is not readable. A tint on the emissive rings, core and crystals would fix it.
2. Dome glass reads as milky opaque; nothing is visible inside (no core sphere, no interior glow).
3. The drum has no openings: no doors at the four stair positions, no window band between pilasters.
4. The four stairs are only a pale mark on the platform edge; the balustrade is not readable.
5. Crystal clusters are single cones; a cluster of two or three unequal spikes would match the word.
