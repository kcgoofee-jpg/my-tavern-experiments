## What matches the setting
- Three-band lab block with a flat roof, rooftop pole with double aether ring, entrance canopy.
- Circular constraint field with double dashed ring and hexagonal pad, test rack with dark armor plates and cyan lines.
- Four field lamps, brown crates, one equipment cabinet, background shed silhouettes and trees.

## Defects (each one is addressed in the fix stage)
1. **No readable storeys or entrance.** The facade is three slabs with two glowing white strips; no door, no window
   rhythm, no floor lines. The white strips are flat emissive bars, not glazing with mullions. Reads as a slab, not a
   3-storey lab.
2. **Hexagonal lock mark is illegible.** Under the canopy it is a white square blob, not the hexagon lock motif.
3. **Guide tube reads as a bead chain.** The "overhead guide tube" from the building to the rack is a thin arc of
   dots, not a tube on supports; it also visually ends in mid-air at the building wall.
4. **Test rack blocks the entrance in c2.** The rack stands directly on the building's entrance axis, so the
   front elevation is cluttered and the rack looks like it grows out of the canopy.
5. **Only one container cabinet.** The setting says two, plus a crate stack; there is one large box and a few crates.
6. **Featureless ground and facade.** Flat concrete plate with no joints, stains or markings; no roof equipment
   besides the antenna, no downpipes, vents, or expansion joints on the walls.

## Not a defect
Neutral by design: no people, no text, no weapon modelling (`docs/rejected.md`).
