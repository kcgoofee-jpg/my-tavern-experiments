## r1 defects

1. Parterres — fixed: gravel, box borders, scroll beds, white roses, lavender; read as formal gardens at map scale.
2. Golf-course lawn — fixed: mown lawn only round the house; the rest is park / meadow tones with gentle hills and dells,
   three walks, specimen trees and wood clumps.
3. Tree variety — fixed: oak, cedar, copper beech, lime, birch, stone pine; feathered wood edges.
4. Rune bands / cores — fixed: thin, dim cyan rings; small cores.
5. Water cone and fall — fixed: the clear-water cone hangs under the south overhang and reads in both views; the lake
   overflow fall is visible under the north rim. (Their board anchors sit inside the transparent meshes, so the occlusion
   test drops them; the features are visible in the images.)
6. Rock — partly: crystals fewer and smaller; the cracked-plate pattern of the shared rock shader is still visible close up.
7. Water tower — fixed: classical colonnaded drum and glass condenser dome.
8. Lake shore — fixed: dark shore grass, grey stones, reed clumps.

## Remaining (not blocking)

- The water cone material reads a little opaque / pale; switch to clearer glass for the final.
- Rock plates on the underside (shared `rock_v17` look, same as the other islands).
