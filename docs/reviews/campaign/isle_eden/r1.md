## Defects

1. **Parterres read as tennis courts.** The four parterres are flat magenta squares; hedges invisible at map scale. Make them
   embroidered box parterres: dark green scroll / quarter beds, gravel paths, white-rose and lavender accents, no red slabs.
2. **Island reads as a golf course.** ~70 % of the top is flat, uniform bright lawn; the manor looks small in it. Needs a
   landscape park: meadow / long-grass tones, mown paths, specimen trees, more wood clumps, gentle relief (dells, a
   ha-ha bank), so that lawn is only near the house.
3. **Trees are broccoli clumps.** Oak crowns are uniform dark balls in blobs; add height / colour variation (lighter
   greens, copper beech, pines), feather wood edges.
4. **Underside rune bands** are thick flat white tubes and the three cores are blown-out white balls; they dominate the
   underside. Thinner, dimmer cyan rings; smaller, bluer cores.
5. **Water cone (R12) not visible** and the **lake overflow fall** is a faint wisp; the cone hangs where the main cone hides
   it. Move it to the front half, larger; make the main fall wider and brighter; add the rim spill visible from the south.
6. **Rock surface:** cracked-mud pattern with zebra strata over the whole underside; crystals read as icicles. Softer
   limestone (fewer cracks, broad ledges), fewer, smaller crystals.
7. **Water tower reads as a lighthouse.** Give it a classical form: colonnaded drum under the glass condenser, lower
   proportion.
8. Lake shore is a harsh white paved ring — use stone edging + reeds / planting, not a 6 m white band.
9. The ward (C6) is invisible, as intended (R10); the platform sill is too small to read — acceptable, keep as is.
