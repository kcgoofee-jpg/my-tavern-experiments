## Defects

1. **No wood (R4).** ~150 small trees (crown r ≈ 4 m) on a 430 m island read as a sparse orchard; the brief is an
   autumn wood with a few bare trees. Needs several hundred trees in dense clumps, crowns 5–8 m, clearings only round
   the house, garden and drive.
2. **Ground colour.** The leaf-litter material renders as pale desert sand, the droop-end "rough" zone as blue-grey;
   neither reads as leaf-covered woodland floor. Darker, warmer litter (rust / umber) with grass between.
3. **Slope invisible (R1).** 46 m over 430 m looks flat at 35°; the drooping north-west end must visibly fall away:
   steeper curve near the tip.
4. **Broken tip reads as a saw cut (R9).** The break is one flat horizontal disc; it needs a jagged, tilted fracture
   surface with protruding shards.
5. **Underside strata "zebra"** (high-contrast wavy bands) and the dry-fall moss is a straight flat green ribbon down
   the cone — replace with a weathered streak that follows the cliff and fades out.
6. Crown colours are washed-out grey; "desaturated" should still read as autumn (rust / ochre) at map scale.
