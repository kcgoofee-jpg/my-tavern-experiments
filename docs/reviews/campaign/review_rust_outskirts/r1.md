## What matches the setting
- Six or seven rows of low corrugated-iron sheds in rust, galvanised grey, faded blue and green; stacked attic storeys on some;
  main lane and side lanes, a four-legged water tower with conical top as the only high point, lamp poles, river along the north
  edge with a broadleaf tree line, skyline silhouettes beyond; no figures or text.

## Defects (polish only, not blocking)
1. The dirt plate has a hard rectangular edge with no transition into the surrounding ground.
2. The river is a flat pale ribbon with no banks, reeds or bridge; it reads as a paved strip.
3. The water tower tank is clean red-brown; "rusted" would want streaks and patches of another metal tone.
4. Lane clutter (crates, drums, plank piles, tarpaulin eaves) is sparse in c2; the lane is wide and clean.
5. Lane pavement has a smooth grey overlay with a hard edge on the dirt; it needs ragged edges.

## Not a defect
Neutral by design: no people, no text or signs, no weapons (`docs/rejected.md` #5, #6). The three named outer-ring facilities are
separate models.
