## What matches the setting
- Two-storey arcade arc with cornice and pitched roof, sand arena with a stepped stand, four tents, a central rotunda, a
  giant wheel, nine lamp posts with a string of lights; no text or figures.

## Defects (each one is addressed in the fix stage)
1. **Washed-out image.** Paving, ring road and tents are all near-white on a pale sky; the ring reads as a glowing white
   band and the tents as white blobs. Exposure is too high and the stone tints too light.
2. **A giant grey disc.** The 215 m paved disc has no edge, kerb or planting, so the scene sits on a featureless plate.
3. **The arcade is a blank wall behind columns.** The ground floor has no dark arch bays, so it reads as a wall with
   sticks in front of it, not an arcade.
4. **Tents are indistinct.** Only two of the four tents read as coloured; the plain ones vanish against the ground, and
   none has a door opening or ridge trim.
5. **Lawns and street trees are invisible** from every camera (setting: lawns and avenue trees along the central road).
6. **Wheel is an olive-grey lattice** with no contrast against the sky; gondolas are tiny.

## Not a defect
Neutral by design: no people, no text or emblems (`docs/rejected.md`). The background tower boxes are the existing
city context, not part of the exported groups.
