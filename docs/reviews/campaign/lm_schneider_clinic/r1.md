3. The intercom pillar is barely visible, and the forecourt gate is shown only as two half-leaves.
4. The conservatory is transparent enough to show its plants but has no door link to the main house.
5. The whole board is very bright and cool; a warmer, softer afternoon light would suit a discreet private clinic.
