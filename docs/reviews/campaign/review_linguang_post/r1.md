## What matches the setting
- Two-storey house: stone ground floor, plastered upper floor, slate roof, two chimneys, shuttered windows, lit door.
- Three-storey square tower with parapet at the west end, walled stone-paved court, double gate with gate-post lamps,
  gabled carriage house on the west wall, well, two court lamps, shrubs along the north wall, no signs or heraldry.

## Defects (polish only, not blocking)
1. The gate leaves are flat dark panels; the setting says wrought iron (no bars, no scrollwork).
2. The outside ground is a hard-edged brown plate; feather it into the grass and add a track to the gate.
3. Tower parapet is low and windows are plain dark rectangles; a projecting cornice or lookout slit would read better.
4. The court paving is uniform; a worn path from gate to door would add life.
5. Carriage house door openings are dark voids without doors or a cart.

## Not a defect
Neutral by design: no people, no text, flags or emblems, no weapons (`docs/rejected.md` #5, #6).
