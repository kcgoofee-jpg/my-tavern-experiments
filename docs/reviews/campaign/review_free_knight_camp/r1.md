## Defects (polish only, not blocking)
1. The ground is a hard-edged rectangular plate with a dark green frame; a camp has no edge like that. Feather the mud into
   the grass and break the outline.
2. Everything is small and far apart: tents are about a tenth of the plate width, so the camp reads as sparse rather than lived-in.
3. Tents are pale grey-white and read as one colour; the red ones are the only relief.
4. No cart ruts, puddles or scattered gear on the mud, although the setting lists ruts.
5. The big-tent porch pediment is not readable in c2; the stalls have no counters or goods visible from c1.
