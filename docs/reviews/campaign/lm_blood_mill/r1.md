## What matches the setting
- Two-bay brick mill hall with a corrugated pitched roof, buttress-like pilasters and high windows, a rusted millstone gear on the east gable
  above a big iron door, a soot-black brick chimney, a semi-buried round pit hall with an iron-lattice dome and four flood lamps, a red
  lamp and a tarpaulin awning at the door, a chain-and-post queue lane, fenced yard with pallets, drums, tyre stacks and crates, gravel and
  asphalt with puddles, shack silhouettes around; no people, no text, no blood or weapons.

## Defects (polish only, not blocking)
1. Background shacks read as pale white boxes on a pale ground; a darker tint would sit them behind the yard.
2. Puddles are small mirror patches; a few larger wet patches near the door would sell the lower-layer mood.
3. Queue chains are thin and hard to see from c1; the lane could use heavier posts or a painted queue line.
4. The pit dome shows sky through the lattice from the west; the dark floor helps, a mesh skin would help more.
5. Whole scene is cool and bright; a warmer, dimmer light would suit an unlit fight pit better (the red lamp barely reads by day).

## Not a defect
Neutral by design: no people, no text or signs, no gore or weapons (`docs/rejected.md` #5, #6). The interior of the pit is not modelled.
