## Defects (not blocking; recorded for a later polish pass)
1. Background sheds read as floating white boxes on the flat ground plane (c1, upper left); they need a dark roof or fade.
2. A pale tilted slab sits under the crane in c2 and looks like a clipping glitch rather than a loading platform.
3. Containers are plain corrugated boxes: no door end, no corner castings, no ribs.
4. The external steel stair on the office is not readable from either camera.
5. Rolling stock has no couplers or bogies detail; the cars look set on a dark plinth.
6. Ground is one flat gravel tone: no ballast bed under the rails, no wheel-track wear.

## Not a defect
