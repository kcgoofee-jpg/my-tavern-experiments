## Defects

1. **Ward reads as chicken wire.** Two dense hex domes paint a bright white honeycomb over the whole island; the brief is a
   thin cold edge whose thickness shows only at the rim. Keep the grid almost invisible on top, put the glow in a rim band.
2. **Forest is a plantation.** Identical teal cones on a regular 6.5 m grid; too blue-grey and too even. Needs height /
   crown variation, clustering, darker ink-green, occasional gaps with black rock.
3. **Black ridges invisible (R2).** Trees cover the crests; expose bare black rock along the ridges (wider, taller).
4. **House lost (R4).** A pale grey slab; the roof should read as one long dark ridge with tall chimneys.
5. **Underside:** zebra strata on the spikes and body; spikes too smooth and regular in section. Rougher, darker basalt,
   more facets.
