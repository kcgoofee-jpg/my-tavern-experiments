**T2 — Pickup patterns (`map/core/pickup.mjs`, ≤ 300 lines).** Before → after:
- Verb lists: `VERBS` (normal, Chinese) unchanged; new `EN_VERBS` = the six English forms that are in `VERBS` today (move
  them out of `VERBS`; `VERBS` keeps exporting the union for callers that read it — check with `grep -rn "VERBS" map tests`);
  new `STRICT_VERBS = ['获得', '得到', '拿取']`; new `STRICT_EN = ['obtains', 'obtained', 'gets', 'got', 'takes', 'took',
  'receives', 'received', 'acquires', 'acquired']`.
- **I-08:** the Chinese patterns (`RX_QUOTED`, `RX_BARE`, `RX_BA`) are built from Chinese verbs only. The English pattern
  becomes `<verb>\s+(?:(?:a|an|the|some|his|her|their|my|your|its)\s+)?(<name>)` with `<name>` starting with a letter;
  `tidyEn` also strips a leading determiner. After: `names('Mara picked up the Brass Key.')` → `['Brass Key']`,
  `names('I grabbed an apple and left')` → `['apple']`.
- **Strict class:** a strict verb's hit counts only when the object is quoted, has a measure word (`QUANT_RE`), or is a
  known name (the known path: `KNOWN_VERB` includes the strict verbs). The disposal form `把 / 将 + noun + 拿取` counts like
  the normal class (`RX_BA` includes `拿取`, not `获得` / `得到`). English strict verbs count only with a quoted name
  (any `QUOTE_PAIRS` pair, also `"…"`) or a known name right after the optional determiner.
- **Never-forms** (design §7.3), one function `blocked(text, at, verb)` applied to every hit (every class, P-12), with the
  clause = the span between the previous and the next of `。！？；…!?;` / line break around the verb:
  - negation: a word of `NEG = ['没有', '没能', '无法', '不能', '没', '未', '不', '别']` ends within the 4 characters before
    the verb inside the clause, unless that `不` starts one of `NEG_EXCEPT = ['不由得', '不由', '不禁', '不得不', '不一会',
    '不久', '不料', '不觉', '不住']`; English: `not`, `n't`, `never`, `no longer` within the 3 words before the verb;
  - question: the clause ends with `？` / `?`, or its last character before the clause end is `吗` / `呢` / `么`, or it
    starts with `是否` / `能否` / `有没有` / `要不要`; English: the clause ends with `?`;
  - dialogue: the verb index lies strictly inside a quote span (`“…”`, `「…」`, `『…』`, `"…"` paired left to right); a span
    that opens right after the verb (+ aspect, + spaces) is the quoted-name form, not dialogue;
  - intention / condition: in the clause before the verb `想`, `要`, `打算`, `准备`, `试图`, `企图`, `希望`, `如果`, `要是`,
    `假如`, `若` (Chinese); `want to`, `wants to`, `try to`, `tries to`, `if`, `would`, `will` (English, word-bounded);
  - potential complement: `得到` whose previous character is one of `看听想做找买办猜闻感觉等赶追吃用见`;
  - compound: `获得` followed by `者` or `感`.
- `NOT_ITEMS` gains (Chinese) `认可 回应 回报 好处 帮助 支持 原谅 安慰 满足 乐趣 成就 成功 胜利 荣誉 名声 地位 权力 知识
  技能 能力 启发 结果 进展 同意 批准 允许 保证 关注 青睐 赏识 体验 收获 平静 安宁 休息` and a new `NOT_ITEMS_EN` (`chance`,
  `opportunity`, `attention`, `lead`, `breath`, `look`, `moment`, `hint`, `idea`, `news`, `message`, `hold`); English names are
  compared lower-cased after the determiner strip.
- `scan` keeps its signature and fact shape; new option `o.vocab` (T3). Authority stays `verified`.
- The tests of §6 list every sentence with its result before → after; the positive sentences of
