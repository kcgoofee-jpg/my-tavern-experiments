Plan `docs/plans/spatial-os.md` §5 Stage C, S6 (tab registry `app/tabs.mjs`; the characters tab adapts to the level).

frozen-logic parity test), and the present group is split into sections by the node tree (design §4). S6-2 (stash) and
S6-3 (Items tab) build on the registry.
